12 407-E:1 Granite State Home Mitigation and Resiliency Program. There is hereby established
13 the Granite State home mitigation and resiliency program (program), which shall be implemented

15 407-E:2 Purpose and Policy. The purpose of this chapter is to establish a program to provide
16 financial grants to homeowners to mitigate residential real property against loss from severe
17 weather events, including but not limited to those involving rain, wind, snow, ice, sleet, hail,
18 tornadoes, hurricanes, and microbursts. The intent of this chapter is to provide New Hampshire
19 residents the opportunity to mitigate the risk to their homes from severe weather events, thereby
20 reducing claim severity, frequency of claims, and insurance premiums for New Hampshire
21 homeowners. This chapter does not create an entitlement for property owners and does not obligate
22 the state to pay for property to be inspected, constructed, improved, mitigated, or retrofitted. The
23 programis subject tolegislativeappropriations, thereceiptof federal grants ormoney, or thereceipt
24 ofothersourcesoffunding.

AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill provides that, if an employer sponsoring a self-funded health benefit plan authorizes
submission of its claims data to the state's comprehensive health care information system, then the
insurance commissioner shall provide that employer access to the claims data for that employer-
sponsoredplan.
The bill also establishes the Granite State home mitigation and resiliency program in the
insurance department to provide financial grants to homeowners to mitigate loss from severe
weatherevents.

AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill provides that, if an employer sponsoring a self-funded health benefit plan authorizes
submission of its claims data to the state's comprehensive health care information system, then the
insurance commissioner shall provide that employer access to the claims data for that employer-
sponsoredplan.
The bill also establishes the Granite State home mitigation and resiliency program in the
insurance department to provide financial grants to homeowners to mitigate loss from severe
weatherevents.
