AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill invalidates out-of-state driver's licenses issued specifically to undocumented
immigrants, and prohibits the director of the department of motor vehicles from issuing or renewing
a driver's license for a person with a pending asylum claim, notwithstanding employment
authorization.

AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill invalidates out-of-state driver's licenses issued specifically to undocumented
immigrants, and prohibits the director of the department of motor vehicles from issuing or renewing
a driver's license for a person with a pending asylum claim, notwithstanding employment
authorization.
