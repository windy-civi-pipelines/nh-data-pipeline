2
3 ANACT providing criminal and civil penalties for the transporting of an unemancipated
4 minorinordertoobtainasurgicalprocedurewithoutparentalpermission.
5
6 Amendthebillbyreplacingallaftertheenactingclausewiththefollowing:
7
8 1 NewSection; CriminalCode;IllegalTransportingofaMinorfor aSurgicalProcedure. Amend
9 RSA633byinsertingaftersection4thefollowingnewsection:
10 633:4-a IllegalTransportingofaMinorforaSurgicalProcedure.
11 I. A person is guilty of a class A misdemeanor if such person intentionally transports a
12 unemancipated minor within this state for the purpose of obtaining a surgical procedure for the
13 minorwithouttheexpresswrittenconsentoftheminor'sparentorguardian.
14 II. A person shall be guilty of a class B felony if the person is convicted of violating this
15 sectionandhaspreviouslybeenconvictedof2ormoreoffensesunderparagraphI.
16 III. For the purpose of this section, an unemancipated minor shall mean any minor child
17 under 18 years of age who is not married or has not by court order otherwise been freed from the
18 care,custody,andcontrolofhisorherparents.
19 IV. Thissectiondoesnotapplyto:
20 (a) Theparentsorlegalguardianoftheunemancipatedminor;
21 (b) Apersonwhohasobtainedthewritten,notarizedconsentofthe
22 unemancipatedminor'sparentorlegalguardian;
23 (c) A common carrier transporting passengers in the course and scope of their business;
24 or
25 (d) Anambulancedriveroroperatorandanycorrespondingemergency
26 medicalservicespersonnelactingwithinthecourseandscopeoftheirduties.
27 V. It shall not be a defense to a prosecution under this section that the unemancipated
28 minorconsentedtotheactionsprohibitedbyparagraphI.
29 2 New Chapter; Private Right of Action for Transporting a Minor for a Surgical Procedure
30 ContrarytoLaw. AmendRSAbyinsertingafterchapter507-Hthefollowingnewchapter:
31 CHAPTER507-I
32 PRIVATERIGHTOFACTIONFORTRANSPORTINGAMINOR

certaincircumstancesfollowingaviolationofthecriminalprohibition.
