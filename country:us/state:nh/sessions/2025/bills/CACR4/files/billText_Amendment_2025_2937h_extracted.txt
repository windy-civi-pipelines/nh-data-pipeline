5 legal resident of the state of 18 years of age and upwards shall have an equal right to vote in any
6 election. Every person shall be considered [an inhabitant] a qualified voter for the purposes of
7 voting in the town, ward, or unincorporated place where he has his domicile only if they are 18
8 years of age and upwards and actually reside in the place they claim as a domicile. No
9 person shall have the right to vote under the constitution of this state who has been convicted of
10 treason, bribery or any willful violation of the election laws of this state or of the United States; but
11 the supreme court may, on notice to the attorney general, restore the privilege to vote to any person
12 who may have forfeited it by conviction of such offenses. The general court shall provide by law for
13 voting by qualified voters who at the time of the biennial or state elections, or of the primary
14 elections therefor, or of city elections, or of town elections by official ballot, are absent from the city
15 or town of which they are inhabitants, or who by reason of physical disability are unable to vote in
16 person, in the choice of any officer or officers to be elected or upon any question submitted at such
17 election. Voting registration and polling places shall be easily accessible to all persons including
18 disabled and elderly persons who are otherwise qualified to vote in the choice of any officer or
19 officers to be elected or upon any question submitted at such election. The right to vote shall not be
20 denied to any person because of the nonpayment of any tax. Every inhabitant of the state, having
21 theproperqualifications,hasanequalrighttobeelectedintooffice.
