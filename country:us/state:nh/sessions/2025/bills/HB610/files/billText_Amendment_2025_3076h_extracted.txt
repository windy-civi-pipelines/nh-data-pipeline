1 I. There is established the residential ratepayers advisory board which shall provide
2 oversight of the office of the consumer advocate as established by RSA 363:28 and ensure that the
3 consumer advocate shall focus exclusively on assuring that residential utility customers receive safe
4 and reliable service at the lowest possible cost while maximizing customer freedom and autonomy.
5 Theadvisoryboardshallconsistofthefollowingpublicmembers:
6 (a) Three members appointed by the speaker of the house. One shall represent the
7 interestsofresidential ratepayers;one shallrepresent theinterestsof theelderly; andone shallbe a
8 memberofthepublic.
9 (b) Three members appointed bythesenatepresident. One shall represent theinterests
10 of residential ratepayers; one shall represent the interests of the disabled; and one shall represent
11 environmentalconcerns.
12 (c) Three members appointed by the governor and council. One shall represent the
13 interests of persons of low income; one shall represent the interests of small business owners; and
14 oneshallrepresenttheinterestsofresidentsoflow-incomehousing.
15 II. Members shall serve 3-year terms. In the event of death or resignation of any member of
16 the advisory board, the person or entity that made the original appointment shall appoint a
17 successor, and the successor appointed to the vacancy shall serve for the remainder of the unexpired
18 term. The members of the board shall receive no compensation but shall be entitled to
19 reimbursementformileageatthesamerateprovidedforstateemployees.
20 III. Theboardshallelectannuallyachairpersonfromamongitsmembership.
21 IV. Theboardshallreceiveadministrativesupportfromtheofficeoftheconsumeradvocate.
22 V.(a) The board shall meet at least quarterly and at the call of the chairperson or 3 board
23 members. The consumer advocate shall be present for all board meetings to inform the board of the
24 actionsoftheofficeoftheconsumeradvocateandtorespondtotheboard'sinquiries.
25 (b) Except as pertains to any end user of an excepted local exchange carrier or services
26 provided to such end user, the board shall advise the consumer advocate on matters concerning
27 residentialratepayers.
28 (c) Prior to the expiration of the consumer advocate's term, the board shall recommend
29 to the governor and council whether to reappoint the consumer advocate. If the board does not
30 recommend reappointment or the governor and council do not accept the board's recommendation to
31 reappoint,theboardshallthenrecommend3personstothegovernorandcounciltofilltheposition.
32 (d) After providing notice and opportunity for a hearing to the consumer advocate, the
33 oversight board may by two-thirds vote recommend that the governor seek the removal of the
34 consumer advocate for failure to discharge the powers, duties, and limitations set forth in RSA
35 363:28. A failure to discharge the powers, duties, and limitations set forth in RSA 363:28 shall be
36 deemedadequategrounds forremoval of theconsumeradvocatepursuant toRSA4:1, and aremoval
