3 1 Authorization to Perform Ophthalmic Laser Procedures. Amend RSA 327 by inserting after
4 section6-cthefollowingnewsection:
5 327:6-d AuthorizationtoPerformOphthalmicLaserProcedures.
6 I. Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, an optometrist licensed under this
7 chapter shall be authorized to perform the following ophthalmic laser procedures upon certification
8 bytheboard:
9 (a) Lasercapsulotomy.
10 (b) Lasertrabeculoplasty.
11 (c) Laserperipheraliridotomy.
12 II. The board shall adopt rules under RSA 541-A establishing the criteria for certification of
13 anoptometristtoperformophthalmiclaserprocedureswithproficiency,including:
14 (a) Establishmentofminimumeducation,training,andliveexperiencerequirementsfor
15 alicenseetoperformsuchprocedureswithproficiency;
16 (b) Criteriaforrequiringproctoringandcriteriaforproctors;and
17 (c) Outcome reporting requirements for such procedures performed by certificate
18 holders.
19 III. No optometrist shall perform ophthalmic laser procedures under this section unless the
20 optometrist has submitted evidence of satisfactory completion of all requirements for specific
21 proceduresandtheboardhascertifiedtheoptometristasqualified.
22 IV.(a) To be certified by the board to perform ophthalmic laser procedures as specified in
23 paragraphI,anoptometristlicensedunderthischaptershall:
24 (1) Completetherequiredapplicationdesignatedbytheboard;and
25 (2) Submit evidence of successful completion of a laser course approved by the board
26 that includes didactic and clinical training relative to the ophthalmic laser procedures set forth in
27 paragraphI;and
28 (3) Demonstrate clinical proficiency in performing the specified ophthalmic laser
29 procedure on a living human eye in the presence of, under the direct supervision of, and to the
30 satisfaction of a board-approved proctor. The proctor shall be an ophthalmologist or an optometrist
31 who is authorized to perform the laser procedure. The licensed applicant shall submit to the board

8 (1) Complete the required application for laser certification by endorsement
