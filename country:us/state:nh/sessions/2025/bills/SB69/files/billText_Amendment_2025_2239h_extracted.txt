23 35-B:1 Establishment and Management. Any town, city, county, village district or school
24 district acting through its governing body, may either independently or in conjunction with the
25 United States, the state of New Hampshire, or any other political subdivision acquire land within or
26 without its jurisdiction by gift, purchase or lease, for a public recreation and park area and may
27 prepare, equip, and maintain said land for said purpose. Said town, city, county or district may
28 establish a system of reasonable fees and charges thereon; may conduct and promote leisure-time
29 activities thereon. In connection therewith, any such town, city, county, or district may construct,
30 reconstruct, equip, operate, and maintain any recreation building or recreation support facility; and
31 may employ an administrative officer and such other persons as it deems necessary to carry out the
32 provisions of this chapter. Any town, city, county or district shall have the power to jointly enter
33 into any contract with any organization or organizations for the purpose of conducting leisure-time
34 programs, acquiring recreational lands or facilities, or exercising any other power granted by this
35 chapter. Gifts made pursuant to this section shall be accepted or rejected by a school board

6 8 New Section; Virtual Early Childhood Readiness Family Engagement Program. Amend RSA
7 186byinsertingaftersection70thefollowingnewsection:
8 186:71VirtualEarlyChildhoodReadinessFamilyEngagementProgram.
9 I. Inthissection:
10 (a) "Department"meansthedepartmentofeducation.
11 (b) "Preschoolchild"meansa4-or5-year-oldchildwhoisnotenrolledinkindergarten.
12 (c) "Virtual early childhood readiness family engagement program" means a statewide
13 nonprofit digital initiative aimed at developing school readiness skills for preschool children and
14 fosteringfamilyengagement.
15 II. There is established a virtual early childhood readiness family engagement program to
16 provide early childhood literacy and improve kindergarten readiness among children in New
17 Hampshire. The program shall offer an online, evidence-based early literacy initiative for children 4
18 and 5 years old who are not yet enrolled in kindergarten. The early childhood readiness program
19 shall operate as a statewide initiative, utilizing educational technology and family engagement to
20 develop school readiness skills for preschool children. The program shall deliver age-appropriate
21 literacypreparationinreading,math,andscience.
22 III. The department of education shall, subject to the availability of appropriated funds,
23 providethekindergartenreadinessliteracyprogramtopreschoolchildrenstatewide.
24 IV. Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the department of education may accept
25 and expend unanticipated funds, grants, or donations from state, federal, or other government
26 entities, as well as private and nonprofit sources, for the kindergarten readiness program, as such
27 fundsbecomeavailable.
28 V. Eachyear,thevirtualearlychildhoodreadinessfamilyengagementprogramshallsubmit
29 an annual report to the governor, speaker of the house, and president of the senate, which shall
30 include,butnotbelimitedto:
31 (a) Numberofchildrenparticipatingintheprogram;
32 (b) Numberoffamiliesparticipatingintheprogram;and
33 (c) Numberofprivateandpublicpreschoolprovidersengagedintheprogram.
34 9 EffectiveDate. ThisactshalltakeeffectJuly1,2025.
