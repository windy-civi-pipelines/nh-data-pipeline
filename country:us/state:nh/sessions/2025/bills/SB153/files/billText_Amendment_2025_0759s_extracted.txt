6 Amendthebillbyreplacingallaftertheenactingclausewiththefollowing:
7
8 1 New Paragraph; Expedited Permitting. Amend RSA 236:13 by inserting after paragraph IV-a
9 thefollowingnewparagraph:
10 IV-b. Forexpeditedpermittingofmajorentrancesforresidentialuseof20unitsorgreater:
11 (a) The commissioner of the department of transportation shall revise the department's
12 policyfor the permittingof driveways and other access tothe state highwaysystem to implement an
13 optional program for the expedited permitting of major entrances for residential use of 20 units or
14 greater,within60businessdaysofapprovalofthetrafficimpactstudy.
15 (b) An applicant electing to use the expedited permitting process under this paragraph
16 shallpaya$120perunitnon-refundablepermitfee.
17 (c) The commissioner of the department of transportation, with approval of the governor
18 andcouncil,mayadjustthefeeinsubparagraph(b).
19 (d) In addition to the permit fee in subparagraph (b), the applicant shall pay a retainer
20 as formulated in the department's policy relating to the permitting of driveways and other access to
21 the state highway system, for reasonable expenses incurred by the department for third party
22 professional engineer review and consultation during the expedited permitting process. All third-
23 party review fees shall be tracked and a copy provided to the applicant. All unused retainages shall
24 berefundedtotheapplicantwithin120daysoftheissuanceofthepermit.
25 2 Position Established. There shall be a classified position of business operations specialist
26 established within the department of transportation, compensated under SOC 13, Broad Group 13-
27 1190, Pay Band 6, for the purpose of administering the expedited permitting of major entrances
28 underRSA236:13,IV-b.
29 3 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect90daysafteritspassage.
