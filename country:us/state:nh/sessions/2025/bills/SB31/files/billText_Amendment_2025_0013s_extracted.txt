3 I. Any person who wishes to become a hunting or fishing guide but who has not completed
4 the written and oral board requirements to become a licensed guide as required in RSA 215:2, may
5 purchase an apprentice guide license. Additional requirements for an individual to qualify for the
6 apprentice guide license shall include passing a criminal background check and certification in first-
7 aid and CPR before an application may be submitted. A person holding a valid apprentice guide
8 license, along with the proper license for the activity they will be assisting with, may guide in
9 activities, including hunting or fishing, only when accompanied by a properly licensed guide who is
10 18 years of age or older. The fees for a resident or non-resident apprentice guide license shall be set
11 inrulespursuanttoRSA541-A,asprovidedinRSA215:4.
