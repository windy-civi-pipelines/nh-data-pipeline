3 1 New Paragraph; Taxation; Payment in Lieu of Taxes for Renewable Generation Facilities;
4 Transition to Payment of State Education Property Tax. Amend RSA 72:74 by inserting after
5 paragraphVIIthefollowingnewparagraph:
6 VIII. Inthecaseofanelectricgeneratingfacilitythatisexemptfromtheutilitypropertytax
7 pursuant to RSA 83-F:1, V(g) and making payments in lieu of taxes to a municipality pursuant to
8 RSA 72:74, if the payment in lieu of tax agreement was in effect as of July 1, 2027, then the facility
9 shall not be liable for payment of the state education tax under RSA 76:3 until that payment in lieu
10 of taxes agreement expires or until July 1, 2032, whichever occurs first. Once payment of the state
11 education tax under RSA 76:3 is required, it may be included in a payment in lieu of taxes
12 agreement pursuant to a new agreement reached under RSA 72:74, or be paid in addition to a
13 payment in lieu of taxes agreement if the underlying agreement has not expired or been reopened
14 andrenegotiated. If,underRSA76:3,thestateeducationtaxispaidseparatelyfromthepaymentin
15 lieu of taxes, then the amount of the state education tax owed by the facility shall be determined by
16 using the imputed value of the facility that is calculated and used by the department of revenue
17 administrationforpurposesofequalizationunderRSA21-J:3,XIII.
18
19 Amendthebillbyreplacingallaftersection3withthefollowing:
20
21 4 UtilityPropertyTax;ExemptionfromEducationTax;ReferenceAdded. AmendRSA83-F:9to
22 readasfollows:
23 83-F:9 Exemption From Education Tax. Persons and property subject to taxation under this
24 chapter shall not be subject to tax under RSA 76:3; provided, however, that nothing in this chapter
25 shall be construed to exempt such persons or property from local school, municipal, district, or
26 countytaxationunderRSA76,exceptasprovidedinRSA72:74,VIII.
27 5 Applicability. ThisactshallapplytotaxperiodsbeginningonorafterApril1,2028.
28 6 EffectiveDate. ThisactshalltakeeffectJuly1,2027.
