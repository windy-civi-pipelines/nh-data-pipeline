3 1 Education;SpecialEducation;StateAid. AmendRSA186-C:18,III(a)-(b)toreadasfollows:
4 III.(a) The state board of education through the commissioner, department of education,
5 shall distribute aid available under this paragraph as entitlement to such school districts as have a
6 special education pupil for whose costs they are responsible, for whom the costs of special education
7 in the fiscal year exceed 3 and 1/2 times the estimated state average expenditure per pupil for the
8 school year preceding the year of distribution. [If in any year, the amount appropriated for
9 distribution as special education aid in accordance with this section is insufficient therefor, the
10 appropriation shall be prorated proportionally based on entitlement among the districts entitled to a
11 grant.] If in any year, the amountappropriated for distribution as special education aid in
12 accordance with this section is insufficient therefor, the appropriation shall be prorated
13 proportionally based on entitlement among the districts entitled to a grant, provided that
14 the department of education shall distribute to the school district not less than 80 percent
15 of the district’s entitlement for catastrophic aid costs in the fiscal year. If there are
16 unexpended funds appropriated under this paragraph at the end of any fiscal year, such funds shall
17 be distributed for court-ordered placements and episodes of treatment under RSA 186-C:19-b. The
18 state may designate up to $250,000 of the funds which are appropriated as required by this
19 paragraph,foreachfiscalyear,toassistthoseschooldistrictswhich,underguidelinesestablishedby
20 rules of the state board of education, may qualify for emergency assistance to mitigate the impact of
21 specialeducationcosts. Thestatemaydesignateuptoanadditional$250,000ofthefundswhichare
22 appropriatedunderthisparagraphforeachfiscalyearforanycommunityof1,000orfewerresidents
23 to mitigate the impact of special education costs when emergency assistance is necessary to prevent
24 significant financial harm to such district or community. Upon application to the commissioner of
25 education, and approval by the commissioner, such funds may be accepted and expended by school
26 districts in accordance with this chapter; provided, however, that if a school district has received
27 emergency assistance funds for certain children with disabilities, it shall not receive special
28 educationaid for thosesamechildrenwithdisabilities. If anyof thefunds designated for emergency
29 assistance under this paragraph are not used for such emergency assistance purposes, the funds
30 shall be used to assist school districts in meeting special education cost increases in their special
31 educationprogramsasprovidedbythisparagraph.

5 2 Education;SpecialEducation;StateAid. AmendRSA186-C:18,IVtoreadasfollows:
6 IV. [The state shall appropriate an amount for each fiscal year to assist special education
7 programs that are statewide in their scope, and that meet the standards for such programs
8 established by the state board of education. Funds under this paragraph shall be administered and
9 distributed by the state board of education through the commissioner.] The amount necessary to
10 fund special education aid under this section is hereby appropriated to the department
11 from the education trust fund created under RSA 198:39. The governor is authorized to
12 draw a warrant from the education trust fund to satisfy the state's obligation under this
13 section. Such warrant for payment shall be issued regardless of the balance of funds
14 available in the education trust fund. If the balance in the education trust fund, after the
15 issuance of any such warrant, is less than zero, the state comptroller shall transfer
16 sufficient funds from the general fund to eliminate such deficit. The commissioner of the
17 department of administrative services shall inform the fiscal committee and the governor
18 and council of such balance. This reporting shall not in any way prohibit or delay the
19 distributionofpayments.
20 3 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect60daysafteritspassage.

AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill removes the requirement that catastrophic special education funds be prorated among
the school districts entitled to such aid and requires that disbursements for special education to a
school district shall be at least 80 percent of the district’s entitlement for catastrophic aid costs in
thefiscalyear.
