14 194:62 Declaration of Intent. The intent of this subdivision is to provide a targeted, temporary
15 fiscalstabilizationmechanismthroughtheestablishmentofaschooldistrictadequacyrevolvingloan
16 fund administered by the state treasury to assist the district with cash flow in anticipation of
17 adequacy payments owed to them by the state pursuant to RSA 198:40-a and paid under the
18 schedulesetforthinRSA198:42.
19 194:63 School District Adequacy Revolving Loan Fund. There is hereby established in the
20 department of treasury a fund to be known as the school district adequacy revolving loan fund,
21 which shall be kept separate and distinct from all other funds. Moneys in the fund shall be
22 nonlapsing and shall be continually appropriated to the treasury, and shall be used to provide loans
23 toschooldistricts.
24 194:64 OutstandingLoanAmount. Atnotimeshallthetotaloutstandingamountofloansmade
25 under this subdivision exceed 75 percent of total adequacy funding to be distributed to school
26 districts,pursuanttoRSA198:41,inanygivenyear.
27 194:65 Certification of Disclosure. Notwithstanding any other provision of law to the contrary,
28 the school district adequacy revolving loan fund shall be considered an allowable use of the
29 educationtrustfundpursuanttoRSA198:39. Moneysmaybeappropriatedfromtheeducationtrust
30 fund to the school district adequacy revolving loan fund, with such funds then being loaned to school
31 districts. Prior to the school district drawing upon the adequacy revolving loan fund, the governing
32 bodyof the school district shall authorize such use of the fund by an affirmative vote of a majority of

1 its members present and voting. Additionally, prior to drawing any funds from the revolving loan
2 fund, the school board and superintendent shall file with the department of education a certification
3 disclosing any activity in the preceding 2 fiscal years relative to reimbursement anticipation notes
4 issued pursuant to RSA 198:20-d. The governor is authorized to draw a warrant from the education

7 I. Seventy-five percent of total adequacy funding to be distributed to the applying school

11 andRSA198:42,I.
12 194:66 Loan Terms and Interest. Loans issued from the school district adequacy revolving loan
13 fund shall bear interest at a rate established by the state treasurer, which shall be based on the
14 state’scurrentcostofborrowing. Aquorumofaschooldistrict'sgoverningbody,thecommissionerof
15 the department of education, and the state treasurer shall all agree to the terms of the loan which
16 shall include terms requiring the school district to report monthly deficit, status, cashflows, audits
17 andstatusofanyoutstandingauditstothetreasury,thedepartmentofrevenueandthedepartment
18 of education, and timely submit all required reports consistent with RSA 198:4-d to the department
19 ofeducation. WhenanadequacypaymentisduetobedistributedpursuanttoRSA198:42:
20 I. The portion of the adequacy payment equivalent to the outstanding principal and interest
21 balanceoftherevolvingloanshallfirstbecreditedagainstsuchbalance;and
22 II. Anyremainingadequacypaymentshallthenbedistributedtoschooldistricts.
23 III. If the commissioner of the department of education and the state treasurer determine
24 thataschooldistrictfailtocomplywithloantermspursuanttothissection,aschooldistrict’sability
25 to receive future loan funds shall be withheld until the school district complies with required loan
26 terms received and accepted by the commissioner of the department of education and the state
27 treasurer.
28 194:67 LoanConditions.
29 I. Funding under the school district adequacy revolving loan fund shall be available to any
30 school district that makes a request and receives approval from the commissioner of the department
31 ofeducation,thestatetreasurer,andthejointfiscalcommitteeofthegeneralcourt.
32 II. Interest rates for fundingreceived under theschool district adequacyrevolvingloan fund
33 shallbedeterminedbythestatetreasurer,butshallbenolowerthantheeffectivefederalfundsrate
34 (EFFR)publishedbytheFederalReserveBankofNewYork.
35 III. Funds received under the school district adequacy revolving loan fund may be used to
36 fulfill the obligations under a revenue anticipation note or tax anticipation note, but no school

5 V. No school may submit a new application for funds under the school district adequacy
6 revolvingloanfundafterJune30,2030.
7 194:67 ConditionforAcceptance.
8 I. School districts shall report monthly deficit, status, cash flows, audits, and the state of

10 the commissioner of the department of education, the commissioner of the department of revenue
11 administration, the governor, the president of the senate, and the speaker of the house of

14 fiscalcommitteeofthegeneralcourt,whoshallrequirethatafinancialandperformanceauditofthe

18 adopt the provision of RSA 32:5-b on the ballot at its next annual school district meeting, unless the
19 schooldistricthasalreadyadoptedtheprovisionsofRSA32:5-b.
20 IV. Any school district within a school administrative unit that is comprised on 2 or more
21 school districts and participates in the school district adequacy revolving loan fund shall place a
22 question of whether to adopt the provisions of RSA 194-C:9-a on its local ballot at its next annual
