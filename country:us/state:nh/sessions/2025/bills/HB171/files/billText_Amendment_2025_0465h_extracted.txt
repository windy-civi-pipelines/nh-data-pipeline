3 1 New Section; Moratorium; Study by the Department of Environmental Services. Amend RSA
4 149-Mbyinsertingaftersection9thefollowingnewsection:
5 149-M:9-a MoratoriumonIssuanceofPermitsforNewLandfill.
6 I. Notwithstanding RSA 149-M:9, the department shall not issue any permit to construct or
7 operate a new landfill facility in New Hampshire under RSA 149-M:9. The department may accept
8 applications, evaluate them for completeness, and request more information to make an application
9 complete, but shall not proceed to further evaluation or process any applications, notwithstanding
10 RSA 541-A:29, in order that any evaluation of need, benefit, harm, or appropriateness of the site
11 proposedwillawaitthedevelopmentofnewregulations,data,technologies,andpolicies.
12 II. Nothing in paragraph I shall be construed to prohibit the expansion or modification of
13 any landfill facilities on any site on which, as of December 1, 2022, a Resource Conservation and
14 RecoveryAct (RCRA) Subtitle D landfill exists that has been permitted in accordancewith RSA 149-
15 M:9.
16 III. In this section, the term “site” means a single parcel or adjacent parcels, owned in their
17 entirety by a landfill operator or its affiliates as of December 1, 2022, including a site where one or
18 morepublicutilityeasementstraversethesite.

22 3 EffectiveDate.
23 I. Section2ofthisactshalltakeeffectJuly1,2028.
24 II. Theremainderofthisactshalltakeeffectuponitspassage.
