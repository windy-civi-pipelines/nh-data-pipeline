AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill requires outpatient substance use disorder treatment programs, with certain
exceptions, to be licensed by the department of health and human services. The bill also makes an
appropriation to the department of health and human services to establish a compliance officer
positiontoimplementtherequirementsoftheact.
