3 1 Fetal Death Record; Reporting Required. RSA 5-C:74, I is repealed and reenacted to read as
4 follows:
5 I. For every fetal death, as defined in RSA 5-C:1, XII, which occurs in the state of New
6 Hampshire, a fetal death worksheet shall be prepared and a fetal death report filed within 5 days,
7 pursuanttoRSA290:1-aandRSA5-C:75.
8 (a) When a fetal death occurs in a New Hampshire facility that regularly prepares birth
9 records,afetaldeathreportshallbefiledelectronicallybythefacility.
10 (b) When a fetal death occurs in a New Hampshire facility that does not prepare birth
11 records,apaperfetal deathworksheetshall becompletedand forwarded tothedivisionimmediately
12 by the person in charge of the hospital or institution where the fetal death occurred. The division
13 shallfilethefetaldeathreportelectronically.
14 (c) When a fetal death occurs in New Hampshire outside a hospital or institution, a
15 paper fetal death worksheet shall be completed and forwarded to the division immediately by the
16 physician, APRN, or physician assistant in attendance at or after delivery. The division will file the
17 fetaldeathreportelectronically.
