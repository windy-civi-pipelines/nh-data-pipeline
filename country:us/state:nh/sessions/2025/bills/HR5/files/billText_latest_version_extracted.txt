Raw Text:
HOUSE RESOLUTION NO. 5  Reps. Steven Smith and Simpson offer the following:  RESOLVED, that the members will be paid their legislative salary, as provided in the Constitution, in the month of January 2025, and that mileage of members of the House of Representatives be paid every two weeks during the session.
