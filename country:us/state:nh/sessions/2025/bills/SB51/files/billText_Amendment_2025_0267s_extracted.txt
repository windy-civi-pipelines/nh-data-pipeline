8 vehicles shall pay over said fee to the state treasurer who shall keep the fee in a special fund to be

10 lakerestorationandpreservationmeasures,exclusiveofexoticaquaticweedcontrol,$5ofthefeefor
11 the control of exotic aquatic weeds, and $4 of the fee for the milfoil and other exotic aquatic plants
12 prevention program. The department shall deposit the $4 into a special account within the lake

14 aquatic plants prevention program. The special fund shall be nonlapsing. All funds received under
15 thissectionarecontinuallyappropriatedtothedepartmentforthepurposesofthissubdivision.

17 personnel, consultant services, and equipment as, in its judgment, will control aquatic nuisances in
18 thesurfacewatersofthestateasdefinedinRSA485-A:2.
19 III. The department shall be the agency to receive and utilize federal funds, gifts, or grants
20 fromanypersonorassociation,whichmaybemadeavailableforthepurposesofthissubdivision.
