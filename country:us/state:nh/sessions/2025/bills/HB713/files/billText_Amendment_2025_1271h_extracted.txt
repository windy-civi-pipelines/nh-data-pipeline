3 1 InstallationandMaintenanceofMileMarkers.
4 I. The department of transportation shall install mile markers along the length of Route
5 112, also known as the Kancamagus Highway, from the town of Lincoln to the town of Conway and
6 mayinstallmilemarkersalongRoute112fromthetownofBathtothetownofWoodstock.
7 II. Generally, mile markers shall be installed at regular intervals of not greater than one
8 mile and shall be clearly visible and legible to motorists and emergency personnel. The department
9 oftransportationmayomitmilemarkersalongdenselydevelopedsegmentsofhighway.
10 III. The department of transportation shall maintain the mile markers installed pursuant
11 this section in accordance with, and subject to, the applicable provisions of the Federal Highway
12 Administration’sManualonUniformTrafficControlDevices.
13 IV. Prior to installation the department of transportation shall meet with public officials
14 from the town of Lincoln and the town of Albany to provide information on mile marker design,
15 proposedlocationsandtrafficimpacts.
16 2 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect18monthsafteritspassage.
