3 3 Communications District Planning Committee; Formation and Responsibilities. Amend RSA
4 53-G:2,Itoreadasfollows:
5 I. The governing bodies of 2 or more municipalities, or the governing body of a county,
6 may at their discretion create a special unpaid committee to be known as a communications district
7 planning committee, consisting of at least 2 persons from each municipality appointed by the
8 respective governing bodies. Governing bodies of municipalities located within a proposed
9 county-wide district may vote to include themselvesin this planning committee. Governing
10 bodies of municipalities may vote to join the proposed communications district at a future
11 time pursuant to RSA 53-G:7. The committee shall elect a chairperson, clerk, and treasurer.
12 Members may be reimbursed by the committee for costs of performing duties directly related to the
13 committee.
