6
7 1 Bail and Recognizances; Release of a Defendant Pending Trial. Amend RSA 597:2, III(c) to
8 readasfollows:
9 (c) Except as provided in RSA 597:1-c, a person who is charged with a qualifying
10 offense [homicide under RSA 630; first degree assault under RSA 631:1; second degree assault
11 under RSA 631:2; felony level domestic violence under RSA 631:2-b; aggravated felonious sexual
12 assault under RSA 632-A:2; felonious sexual assault under RSA 632-A:3; kidnapping under RSA
13 633:1; felony level stalking under RSA 633:3-a, VI(a); trafficking in persons under RSA 633:7;
14 robbery under RSA 636:1, III; possession, manufacture, or distribution of child sexual abuse images
15 under RSA 649-A; or computer pornography and child exploitation under RSA 649-B;] shall not be
16 brought before a bail commissioner and shall, upon arrest, be detained pending arraignment before
17 the court. Arraignment shall occur no later than 36 hours after the arrest, excluding weekends and
18 holidays. At the person's appearance before the court, the court shall order that the person be
19 detained pending trial if the court determines by a finding of probable cause that release of the
20 person is a danger to that person or the public. In determining whether release will endanger the
21 safety of that person or the public, the court may consider all relevant and material factors
22 presented pursuant to paragraph VI. If the court does not find probable cause that the person must
23 be detained, the court shall order the person released pursuant to subparagraph I(b), or, if
24 applicable, temporarily detained pursuant to subparagraph I(d). A person arrested for violating the
25 conditionsofhis orherbail foranoffense listedinthissubparagraph,shall behelduntil theycanbe
26 brought before the court at the first available date. If at a subsequent hearing, the court finds
27 probable cause exists that the person violated the conditions of his or her bail for any of the crimes
28 listed in this subparagraph, the defendant shall be held pending trial. For the purpose of this
29 subparagraph,"qualifyingoffense"meansanyofthefollowing:
30 (1) HomicideunderRSA630;
31 (2) FirstdegreeassaultunderRSA631:1;
32 (3) SeconddegreeassaultunderRSA631:2;
