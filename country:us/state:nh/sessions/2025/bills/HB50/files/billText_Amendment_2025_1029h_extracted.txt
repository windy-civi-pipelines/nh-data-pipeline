8 1 Education; Pupils; Discrimination in Public Schools; Prohibition on Teaching Discrimination.
9 RSA193:40,II-Visrepealedandreenactedtoreadasfollows:
10 II. Nothing in this section shall be construed to prohibit discussion by educators, or others
11 as part of a larger course of academic instruction, the historical existence of ideas and subjects
12 identifiedinthissection.
13 III. Prohibitions inthis section applyonly to teaching, instruction, and inculcation targeting
14 members of any racial group, including but not limited to, white people or people of European
15 descent,blackpeopleorpeopleofSub-SaharanAfricandescent,HispanicorLatino.
16 IV. Intentional or knowing violation of this section by an educator shall be considered a
17 violation of the educator code of conduct, which shall justify investigation and, potentially,
18 disciplinarysanctionbythestateboardofeducation.
19 V. Any person claiming to be aggrieved by an intentional or knowing violation of this
20 section, including the attorney general, may initiate a civil action against a school or school district
21 insuperiorcourtforlegalorequitablerelief,orwiththecommissionforhumanrightsasprovidedin
22 RSA354-A:34.
23 VI. Discrimination shall not be justified by special considerations for any racial group. The
24 intent of this section is to equally protect all New Hampshire citizens from discrimination,
25 regardlessofrace.
26 VII. Forthepurposesofthissection,"educator"meansaprofessionalemployeeofanyschool
27 district whose position requires certification by the state board pursuant to RSA 189:39.
28 Administrators,specialists,andteachersareincludedwithinthedefinitionofthisterm.
29 2 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffectuponitspassage.
