3 1 New Subdivision;ProhibitingDifferentiationBased ontheNumber ofBedrooms. Amend RSA
4 674byinsertingaftersection76thefollowingnewsubdivision:
5 ProhibitingDifferentiationBasedontheNumberofBedrooms
6 674:77 Definitions. Inthissubdivision:
7 I. "Residential development" means any housing project that includes one or more dwelling
8 units,regardlessofwhethertheyareintendedforrentorpurchase.
9 II. "Bedroom" means a furnished room with a bed intended primarily for sleeping purposes,
10 unlessotherwisespecifiedbylocalregulations.
11 674:78 Prohibition on Differentiation Based on Number of Bedrooms. If a residential
12 development proposal makes adequateprovisionfor water supplyand sewagedisposal in accordance
13 with RSA 485-A and if applicable, the planning board’s site plan review regulations or subdivision
14 regulations, planning boards may not use the number of bedrooms as cause or criteria to reject the
15 application. Nothing in this section shall be construed to affect the department of environmental
16 services regulations for individual sewage disposal systems. Nothing in this section shall prevent
17 the authority having jurisdiction or building inspector from taking the number of bedrooms into
18 account when determining whether a residential development has adequate water and sewage
19 disposalcapacityinconformancewithRSA485-Apriortoissuance ofabuildingpermitor occupancy
20 permitinaccordancewith676:13,I.
21 2 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect60daysafteritspassage.
