7 2 DiscriminationinPublicSchools;ProhibitiononTeachingDiscrimination. RSA193:40,II-Vis
8 repealedandreenactedtoreadasfollows:
9 II. Nothing in this section shall be construed to prohibit discussion by educators or others,
10 as part of a larger course of academic instruction, of the historical existence of ideas and subjects
11 identified inthis section, or construed tomeanthat educators have any positiveinterest in teaching,
12 instructing,orinculcatingdiscriminationagainstanygroupofpeople.
13 III. Prohibitions in this section shall apply only to teaching, instruction, and inculcation
14 targeting members of any racial group, including but not limited to, white people or people of
15 European descent, black people or people of Sub-Saharan African descent, or Hispanic or Latino
16 people.
17 IV. A report of intentional or knowing violation of this section by an educator shall be
18 considered a possible violation of the educator code of conduct which shall justify investigation and
19 potentiallydisciplinarysanctionbythestateboardofeducation.
20 V. Any person claiming to be aggrieved by an intentional or knowing violation of this
21 section, including the attorney general, may initiate a civil action against a school or school district
22 in superior court for legal or equitable relief, or with the commission for human rights as provided
23 RSA354-A:34.
24 VI. Discrimination shall not be justified by special considerations for any racial group. The
25 intentofthissectionistoequallyprotectallNewHampshirecitizensfromdiscriminationregardless
26 ofrace.
27 VII. Forthepurposesofthissection,"educator"meansaprofessionalemployeeofanyschool
28 district whose position requires certification by the state board pursuant to RSA 189:39.
29 Administrators,specialists,andteachersareincludedwithinthedefinitionofthisterm.
30 3 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffectuponitspassage.
