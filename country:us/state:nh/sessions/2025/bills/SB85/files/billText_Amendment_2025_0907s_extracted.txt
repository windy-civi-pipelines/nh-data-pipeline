7 exceed the limitation prescribed for national banks under federal law, or the following
8 limitations,whichever isgreater: (i)20 percentof the totalof the depository bank'scapital
9 and surplus; or (ii) 25 percent of the total of the depository bank's capital and surplus, if
10 the amount of the liabilities in excess of the limitation in clause (i) is fully secured by
11 obligationsoftheUnitedStatesoflikevalue. Toqualifyforthehigherlimitationinclause
