3 6 Obtaining a Ballot; Affidavit Ballots Removed. RSA 659:13, I(c) is repealed and reenacted to
4 readasfollows:
5 (c)(1) If the voter does not have a valid photo identification, the ballot clerk shall inform
6 the voter that he or she may execute a challenged voter affidavit. The voter shall receive an
7 explanatory document prepared by the secretary of state explaining the proof of identity
8 requirements. If the voter executes a challenged voter affidavit, the ballot clerk shall mark the
9 checklistinaccordancewithuniformproceduresdevelopedbythesecretaryofstate.
10 (2) If the voter executes a challenged voter affidavit, the moderator or the
11 moderator's designee shall take a photograph of the voter and immediately print and attach the
12 photograph to, thus making it a part of, the affidavit form. However, if a photograph was taken
13 under RSA 654:12, then a notation shall be made on the challenged voter affidavit stating that the
14 photographisattachedtothequalifiedvoteraffidavitorswornstatementonthegeneralelectionday
15 registration form. The photograph shall be 2 inches by 2 inches, or larger, and may be in color or in
16 black and white. The moderator or his or her designee who took the photograph and the voter shall
17 thensignthechallengedvoteraffidavit. Themoderatorordesigneeshalldeletethephotographfrom
18 thecamerainthepresenceof thevoter. Ifthemoderator orhisor herdesigneeis unabletotake the
19 voter's photograph due to equipment failure or other cause beyond the moderator's or his or her
20 designee's reasonable control, the voter may execute a challenged voter affidavit without a
21 photograph.
22 (3) If the voter objects to the photograph requirement because of religious beliefs, he
23 or she may execute an affidavit of religious exemption in accordance with RSA 659:13-b, which shall
24 beattestedtobyanelectionofficerandattachedtothechallengedvoteraffidavit.
25 (4) The person entering voter information into the centralized voter registration
26 database shall cause the records to indicate when a voter has not presented a valid photo
27 identificationandhasexecutedachallengedvoteraffidavit.
28 (5) If the voter objects to the photograph requirement because of religious beliefs, he
29 or she may execute an affidavit of religious exemption in accordance with RSA 659:13-b, which shall
30 beattestedtobyanelectionofficerandattachedtothechallengedvoteraffidavit.
