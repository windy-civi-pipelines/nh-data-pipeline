10
11 2 New Section; Funds Paid from Collected Donations. Amend RSA 206 by inserting after
12 section33-gthefollowingnewsection:
13 206:33-h Funds Paid from Collected Donations. The executive director may approve donation
14 collection points and may accept funds paid from such donation collection points within the state of
15 New Hampshire. Notwithstanding any other provision of law to the contrary, the executive director
16 mayacceptandreceivesuchfundswithouttheapprovalofthegovernor,thegovernorandcouncil,or
17 the commission. All moneys received under this section shall be deposited into the fish and game
18 fund established under RSA 206:33 and used solely for the purposes set forth in RSA 206:34-a. The
19 executive director may invest in technology necessary to enable the solicitation and collection of
20 donations.
21 3 FishandGameFund. AmendRSA206:34-atoreadasfollows:
22 206:34-a Use of Fish and Game Funds. All revenues accruing from sales of licenses and
23 permits, donations solicited and collected at donation collection points approved by the
24 executive director, and any other revenue received by the department, and any money reimbursed
25 or granted to the department by the state or federal government for fish, game, and wildlife
26 conservation or related programs shall be used solely for conservation, restoration, management,
27 educational benefit, recreational use, and scientific study of the fish, game, and wildlife resources of
28 the state, including acquisition of property and general administration of RSA title XVIII. Such
29 fundsshallbeusedfornootherpurposes.
