3 483:8-b Local River Management Advisory Committee Grants. There is hereby established a
4 grant program, to be administered by the department of environmental services, for the allocation of
5 funds to local river management advisory committees established under RSA 483:8-a. Funding
6 requests made under this section may be submitted to the department of environmental services by
7 any established local river management advisory committee, detailing resources needed to fulfill its
8 duties as described in RSA 483:8-a. Funding requests may not exceed $10,000 for any local river
9 management advisory committee in any year. The commissioner of the department of
10 environmental services shall disburse the funding to local river management advisory committees
11 with demonstrated need to implement the duties described in RSA 483:8-a, III on a first come, first-
12 servedbasis.
