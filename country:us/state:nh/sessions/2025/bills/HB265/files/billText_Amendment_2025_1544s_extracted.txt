8 1 Access toGovernmental Records and Meetings; Meetings OpentoPublic. Amend RSA 91-A:2,
9 IItoreadasfollows:
10 II. Subject to the provisions of RSA 91-A:3, all meetings, whether held in person, by means
11 of telephone or electronic communication, or in any other manner, shall be open to the public.
12 Except for town meetings, school district meetings, and elections, no vote while in open session may
13 be taken by secret ballot. Any person shall be permitted to use recording devices, including, but not
14 limited to, tape recorders, cameras, and videotape equipment, at such meetings. Minutes of all such
15 meetings, including nonpublic sessions, shall include the names of members, persons appearing
16 before the public bodies, [and] a brief description of the subject matter discussed and final decisions,
17 the start time and end time of the meeting, and name of the person who produced the
18 minutes. The names of the members who made or seconded each motion shall be recorded in the
19 minutes. Subject to the provisions of RSA 91-A:3, minutes shall be promptly recorded and open to
20 publicinspectionnotmorethan5businessdaysafterthemeeting,exceptasprovidedinRSA91-A:6,
21 and shall be treated as permanent records of any public body, or any subordinate body thereof,
22 without exception. Except in an emergency or when there is a meeting of a legislative committee, a
23 notice of the time and place of each such meeting, including a nonpublic session, shall be posted in 2
24 appropriate places one of which may be the public body's Internet website, if such exists, or shall be
25 printed in a newspaper of general circulation in the city or town at least 24 hours, excluding
26 Sundays and legal holidays, prior to such meetings. An emergency shall mean a situation where
27 immediate undelayed action is deemed to be imperative by the chairman or presiding officer of the
28 publicbody,who shallpost anoticeofthetimeand placeof suchmeetingassoonaspracticable, and
29 shall employwhatever further means arereasonablyavailabletoinform thepublic that ameetingis
30 to be held. The minutes of the meeting shall clearly spell out the need for the emergency meeting.
31 When a meeting of a legislative committee is held, publication made pursuant to the rules of the
32 house of representatives or the senate, whichever rules are appropriate, shall be sufficient notice. If
