8 1 New Paragraph; Policy; Transport for Medical and Health-Related Appointments. Amend
9 RSA186:11byinsertingafterparagraphIX-ethefollowingnewparagraph:
10 IX-f. Require School Districts to Adopt a Policy Prohibiting Transportation for Medical and
11 Health-Related Appointments. A school district shall adopt a policy prohibiting any school district
12 personnel from transporting a minor or accompanying a minor during transport, except emergency
13 transport by ambulance, for a medical appointment, mental health appointment or visit that
14 includes any type of mental health evaluation, treatment, or counseling, or any other health-related
15 appointment or visit, without the knowledge and approval of the minor's parent or guardian, unless
16 he or she is following a published school district emergency health or medical emergency protocol or
17 policy.
18 2 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect60daysafteritspassage.
