AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Requires that electric rate schedules for public utilities, rural electric cooperatives, municipal
or county aggregations, and municipal electric utilities charge the same electric rates for
condominium associations that do not permit commercial or business enterprises as they do for
residentialunits.
II. Requires that the billing for all such residential units be at residential rates, even if the
homeownersassociationisthecustomerandevenifsharedmetersareused.
III. Defines the cost allocation for shared residential meter usage for septic systems and well
pumps.
