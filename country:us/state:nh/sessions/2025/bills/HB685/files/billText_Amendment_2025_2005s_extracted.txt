3 674:31 Definition. As used in this subdivision, "manufactured housing" means any structure,
4 transportable in one or more sections, which, in the traveling mode, is 8 body feet or more in width
5 and 40 body feet or more in length, or when erected on site, is 320 square feet or more, and which is
6 built on a permanent chassis and designed to be used as a dwelling with or without a permanent
7 foundation when connected to required utilities, which include plumbing, heating, and electrical
8 heating systems contained therein. Manufactured housing as defined in this section shall not
9 include campers or recreation vehicles as defined in RSA 216-I:1 or RSA 259:84-a; pre-site built
