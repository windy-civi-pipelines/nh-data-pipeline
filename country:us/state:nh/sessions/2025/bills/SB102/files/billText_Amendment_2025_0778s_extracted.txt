8 1 New Section; School Health Services; Informational Materials Regarding Type 1 Diabetes and
9 Certain Communicable Diseases. Amend RSA 200 by inserting after section 40-c the following new
10 section:
11 200:40-d Informational Materials Regarding Type 1 Diabetes and Certain Communicable
12 Diseases.
13 I. The department of education, in consultation with the department of health and human
14 services, shall develop or identify existing informational materials meeting the requirements of
15 paragraph II, relative to type 1 diabetes, measles, mumps, rubella, and chickenpox. The
16 informational materials shall be periodically updated as necessary, and made available to school
17 districts by posting on the department of education’s Internet website. Each school district shall
18 make the informational materials from the department’s website known or otherwise accessible to
19 the parents or guardians of the students enrolled in the school district, in a manner determined by
20 the school district, including, but not limited to, through the district’s website or by other digital
21 means.
22 II. The informational materials may be in the form of links to existing related resources
23 posted to the websites of any entities the department of education deems appropriate, but shall
24 include,atminimum:
25 (a) Adescriptionoftype1diabetes,measles,mumps,rubella,andchickenpox;
26 (b) A description of risk factors and warning signs associated with type 1 diabetes,
27 measles,mumps,rubella,andchickenpox;
28 (c) A recommendation that the parents or guardians consider vaccination against
29 measles,mumps,rubella,andchickenpox;
30 (d) A recommendation that the parents or guardians of students displaying warning
31 signs associated with type 1 diabetes should consult with the student’s primary care provider to
32 determineifscreeningfortype1diabetesisappropriate;
