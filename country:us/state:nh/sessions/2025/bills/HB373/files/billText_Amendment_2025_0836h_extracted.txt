3 1 Choices and Duties of Town Officers; Selectmen; Town Property. Amend RSA 41:11-a, II, to
4 readasfollows:
5 II. The authority under paragraph I shall include the power to rent or lease such property
6 during periods not needed for public use, [provided, however, that any rental or lease agreement for
7 a period of more than one year shall not be valid unless ratified by vote of the town.] subject to the
8 followingprovisions:
9 (a) If the proposed lease is for one year or less, then the selectmen may lease the
10 propertywithoutfurtheractionbythelegislativebody;
11 (b) If the proposed lease is for more than one year but no greater than 5 years,
12 then the legislative body may vote to authorize the selectmen to enter into the proposed
13 leaseforaspecificmunicipalpropertybymajorityvote;or
14 (c) If the proposed lease is for more than 5 years, then the legislative body may
15 vote by a 3/5 majority at an annual or special meeting to grant the selectmen the authority
16 to rent or lease a specific municipal property for an amount of time specified in the
17 question, not to exceed 99 years. Voting on the question shall be by ballot, but the question
18 shall not be placed on the official ballot used to elect officers, except in the case of a
19 legislative body that uses an official ballot form of meeting under RSA 40:13 or under a
20 charter adopted pursuant to RSA 49-D. Polls shall remain open and ballots shall be
21 accepted by the moderator for a period of not less than one hour following the completion
22 ofdiscussiononthequestion.

AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Requires selectmen to attain an approval vote to rent or lease a specified real property owned
bythetownforaperiodofmorethanoneyearandupto5years.
II. Requires selectmen to attain a 3/5 majority ballot vote to rent or lease a specified real
propertyownedbythetownforaperiodofmorethan5yearsandupto99years.
III. Provides that selectmen can enter into leases of any real property owned by the town for up
to one year without action by the legislative body, and that selectmen may be granted universal
authority by an approval vote of the legislative body to enter into leases of any real property owned
bythetownforupto5years.
