5 195:19-a CompositionofCooperativeSchoolBoards.

21 school board members pursuant to this chapter, said election shall be with the use of the non-
22 partisanballotsystemunderRSA669.
23 V. The number of members and composition of cooperative school boards shall be in
24 accordancewiththissectionbyJuly1,2026. Memberswhosetermsexpirepriortothisdateshallbe
