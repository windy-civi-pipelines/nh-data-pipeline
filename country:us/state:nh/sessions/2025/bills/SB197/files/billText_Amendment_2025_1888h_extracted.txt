8 125:13-a HealthServiceRoom.
9 I. The [legislative facilities committee] general court shall equip and maintain a health
10 service room in such location in the state house as may be assigned for such purpose. Said room
11 shall be staffed by a licensed registered nurse employed or contracted by the [legislative facilities
12 committee] general court, and the room shall be kept open [at all times when the state house is
13 open for business] as directed by the chief operating officer of the general court. The expense
14 of the health service room, including a contract nurse, shall be a charge upon the joint expenses
15 appropriationtothegeneralcourt. [Thedepartmentofhealthandhumanservicesmay,intheevent
16 of the absence of the employed nurse, provide from its staff a licensed registered nurse on a
17 temporary basis. The department of health and human services is further authorized, in the event
18 the department cannot provide a licensed registered nurse, to negotiate a contract for the legislative
19 facilities committee with a nursing service for the purpose of staffing the health service room in the
20 event of an absence of the employed nurse. The daily coordination of the contract nurse shall be
21 accomplished jointly with the house chief of staff and the department of health and human services.
22 The commissioner] The chief medical officer of the department of health and human services
23 [shallprovidemedicalsupervisionfortheemployednurse,foranytemporarilyassignednurseand,if
24 necessary, for any contracted nursing service] may provide standing orders for medications
25 requiring a prescription for the employed or contracted nurse. The licensed registered
26 nurse employed or contracted under this section shall act within the scope of his or her
27 practiceunderRSA326-B.
28 II. Thechiefmedicalofficerofthedepartmentofhealthandhumanservices,acting
29 in good faith and with reasonable care, in issuing a standing order, shall not be subject to
30 any criminal or civil liability, or any professional disciplinary action, for any action
31 authorized by this section or any outcome resulting from an action authorized by this
32 section.
