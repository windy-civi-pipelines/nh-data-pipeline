3 1 Lobbyists;ProhibitedActivities. RSA15:5isrepealedandreenactedtoreadasfollows:
4 15:5 ProhibitedLobbyistActivities.
5 I. In this section, "public funds" means a grant or appropriation to, and received by, a state,
6 county,town,city,villagedistrict,unincorporatedplace,orschooldistrict.
7 II. Except as provided in paragraph V, no recipient of a grant or appropriation of public
8 funds may use public funds to lobby or attempt to influence legislation or contribute public funds to
9 any person, partnership, firm, or corporation that is employed to promote or oppose, directly or
10 indirectly,anylegislationpendingorproposedbeforethegeneralcourt.
11 III. Any recipient of a grant or appropriation of public funds that wishes to engage in any of
12 the activities prohibited in paragraph II, or contribute funds to any entity engaged in these
13 activities, shall segregate the public funds in such a manner that such funds are physically and
14 financially separate from any non-public funds that may be used for any of these purposes. Mere
15 bookkeepingseparationofthepublicfundsfromothermoneysshallnotbesufficient.
16 IV. Nothing in this section shall restrict public officials or public employees from testifying
17 before the legislature unless the individual testifying is required to register as a lobbyist in
18 accordancewithRSA15:1.
19 V. A municipality that wishes to engage in the activities prohibited by RSA 15:5, II may
20 permitthoseactivitiesinthefollowingmanner:
21 (a) In a town, the question shall be placed on the warrant of an annual town meeting
22 under the procedures set out in RSA 39:3, and shall be voted on a ballot. In a city, the legislative
23 bodymayvotetoplacethequestionontheofficialballotforanyregularmunicipalelection,or,inthe
24 alternative, shall place the question on the official ballot for any regular municipal election upon
25 submissiontothelegislativebodyofapetitionsignedby5percentoftheregisteredvoters.
26 (b) The selectmen, aldermen, or city council shall hold a public hearing on the question
27 at least 15 days but not more than 30 days before the question is to be voted on. Notice of the
28 hearing shall be posted in at least 2 public places in the municipality and published in a newspaper
29 ofgeneralcirculationatleast7daysbeforethehearing.
30 (c) Thewordingofthequestionshallbesubstantiallyasfollows:
31 "Shall we allow public funds to be used for lobbying, an attempt to influence legislation, participate
32 inpoliticalactivity,orcontributefundstoanyentityengagedintheselobbyingactivities?"
