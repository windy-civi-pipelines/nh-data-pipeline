3 1 New Section; Special Education State Complaints and Findings; Complaint Tracking. Amend
4 RSA186-Cbyinsertingaftersection5thefollowingnewsection:
5 186-C:5-a SpecialEducationStateComplaintsandFindings;ComplaintTracking.
6 I. The department shall establish and maintain an internal tracking system for all state
7 complaints related to special education services. The tracking system shall include, but not be
8 limitedto,thefollowinginformation:
9 (a) Thedatethecomplaintwasfiled.
10 (b) Thecomplainant’scontactinformation.
11 (c) The respondent’s information, includingthe name of the public school involved in the
12 complaint.
13 (d) Thenameofanylawfirmrepresentingtheschooldistrictinthecomplaint.
14 (e) Asummaryoftheallegations.
15 (f) Key dates relevant to the complaint process, including acknowledgment, response,
16 investigation,andresolution.
17 (g) Statusupdatesthroughouttheinvestigationprocess.
18 (h) The outcome of the complaint, including findings of fact and any corrective actions
19 required.
20 II. The department shall make available summaries of all state complaints and their
21 outcomes on the department’s website within 30 days of the issuance of the final decision on the
22 complaint to the complainant. The department shall redact all personally identifiable information
23 (PII)relatedtostudentsand complainantstoensurecompliancewiththeFamilyEducational Rights
24 and Privacy Act (FERPA) and other state or federal privacy laws. The information shall be limited
25 to:
26 (a) Thedatesthecomplaintwasfiled.
27 (b) Asummaryofthecomplaint.
28 (c) Thedeterminationsoffactandconclusionsreachedbythedepartment.
29 (d) Anycorrectiveactionsrequiredortakenbytherespondent.
30 III. The department shall publishan annual report by July1st each year beginning in 2026.
31 The annual report shall be made publicly available on the department’s website and provided to the
32 office of special education advocate, state advisory council on the education of children with
