3 ANACT relativetotheSelectiveServiceComplianceAct.
4
5 Amendthebillbyreplacingallaftertheenactingclausewiththefollowing:
6
7 1 New Subdivision; Selective Service Registration Awareness and Compliance Act. Amend RSA
8 112byinsertingaftersection12thefollowingnewsubdivision:
9 SelectiveServiceRegistrationAwarenessandComplianceAct
10 112:13 Title. This subdivision shall be known as the Selective Service Registration Awareness
11 andComplianceAct.
12 112:14 Application.
13 I. No person who is not in compliance with the Military Selective Service Act as provided in
14 50 U.S.C. app. section 451 et seq. having attained the age of 18 years, shall be eligible for
15 employment by or service to the state or any political subdivision of the state, including all state
16 boards,commissions,departments,agencies,andinstitutions.
17 II. A person who has authorized the department of safety to submit information to the
18 Selective Service System pursuant to RSA 263:5-c shall be considered to be in compliance with the
19 SelectiveServiceActforpurposesofthissection.
20 112:15 Responsibility for Compliance. Any official responsible for hiring employees by the state
21 oritspoliticalsubdivisionsshallassurethemselvesthatapplicantscomplywithRSA112:14.
22 112:16 Exceptions. Theprovisionsofthissubdivisionshallnotapply:
23 I. If the requirement for the person to register under the Military Selective Service Act has
24 terminated,orotherwisebecomeinapplicabletosuchperson;
25 II. If the person is serving or already has served in the military, or has a condition that
26 would,undermilitaryrulesandregulations,precludemilitaryservice;or
27 III. To state-supported institutions of postsecondary higher education or to decisions related
28 tograntingstate-supportedfinancialassistanceforpostsecondaryhighereducation.
29 2 Repeal. RSA187-Asections38through41,relativetotheServiceRegistrationAwarenessand
30 ComplianceAct,arerepealed.
31 3 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect60daysafteritspassage.
AmendmenttoHB55

AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill relocates the Selective Service Registration Awareness and Compliance Act from RSA
chapter 187-A to chapter 112, and removes the state university's requirement to enforce selective
serviceregistration.
